# Plan 0005 — Mobile-first layout

- Spec: `specs/active/0005-mobile-first-layout.md`
- Status: Approved
- Approved by / on: Cemal Karabulaklı, 2026-10-07

**Approach.** Tailwind only, mobile-first, with today's desktop pinned at `lg:` (1024 px).
- Changes that would alter the desktop look are written for phones and reset at `lg:`, or scoped
  with `max-lg:`.
- The only change that reaches desktop is the 12 px text floor (CB-5), which PB-1 allows.
- No new dependency, no new component file, no change to domain, application or server code.
  F-1…F-6 are untouched.

## Files to change or add
1. `src/app.css`
   - **`tap` utility:** below 1024 px, `min-height` and `min-width` 2.75 rem (44 px). One
     class used for every control in CB-4, instead of repeating `max-lg:min-h-11 max-lg:min-w-11`.
   - **Tables (CB-3):** `.prose-map td, th` get `max-sm:min-w-36` (144 px). A narrow table then
     grows wider than its column and scrolls inside its own `overflow-x-auto` box, which already
     exists.
   - **Background (CB-7):** the two radial gradients move from `body` with
     `background-attachment: fixed` to a fixed `body::before` layer (`inset: 0; z-index: -1`).
     The desktop look is the same, and the full-page seam is gone.
   - **Anchors (spec self-critique 1):** `scroll-padding-top: 4.5rem` below 1024 px, so `/#faz-5`
     lands under the pinned bar.
2. `src/routes/+layout.svelte` — header (CB-1)
   - **Below `lg`:** one pinned row (`sticky top-0 z-40`, ≤ 64 px, `bg-bg/90 backdrop-blur`,
     bottom border). It holds:
     - the dragon (28 px) and the site name at 14 px, wrapping to at most 2 lines. Measured in
       Cinzel: "Build Journey" is 115 px, and the space left at 320 px is 118 px;
     - the EN/TR switch as two 44 × 44 links;
     - a `<details>` menu whose `<summary>` is the 44 × 44 "☰" button.
   - **Menu panel:** full width under the bar. All 9 destinations are rows ≥ 44 px with their icon,
     and the current page uses today's green `active()` highlight plus `aria-current="page"`.
   - **Without JavaScript,** `<details>` opens natively and every row is a plain link (PB-4).
   - **With JavaScript,** `afterNavigate` closes the panel (CB-1 "closes when a destination is
     chosen"). Escape closes it too.
   - **From `lg`:** today's `nav` and language markup with exactly today's classes. The nav links
     come from one `{#snippet}`, rendered in both places, so the list exists once.
   - **Main and footer:** `px-4 sm:px-5`, which is unchanged from 640 px.
   - `/switch` stays bare (PB-2).
3. `src/routes/+page.svelte` — home trail (CB-2, CB-4, CB-5, CB-6)
   - **Rail:** `ol` gets `pl-9 lg:pl-16`. Waypoints are `h-8 w-8` on phones and `lg:h-12 lg:w-12`
     on desktop, with the trail line re-centred on them. The rail then takes 36 px, within CB-6's
     48 px limit.
   - **Cards:** `p-4 sm:p-6`.
   - **Scroll and quest rows:**
     - Each row is a `tap` row.
     - On phones the `+100`/`+150` chip sits inline after the title instead of in its own column.
     - The `→` read link is a 44 × 44 `tap`. When the row is too narrow for title plus arrow, the
       arrow wraps onto its own line (`flex-wrap`, title basis 15 rem). A title never drops below
       half the screen (CB-2).
   - **Phase posts:** title and date wrap instead of squeezing, and the links are `tap`.
   - **Chapter banners:** opaque background below `lg`, so the trail line passes behind the text
     instead of through it. "Meet the merchants" is `tap`. Build-log phase labels get
     `whitespace-nowrap`.
   - **"You are here" dragon:** on phones it sits inside the card next to the bubble (`lg:hidden`).
     The absolute one, which today sits off-screen to the left of a phone, becomes
     `hidden lg:block`.
   - **Small text:** all `text-[.7rem]` and `text-[.6rem]` become `text-xs` (12 px).

## Risks & open questions
- **The name might not fit at 320 px with EN/TR and the menu.** I measured it: it fits in 2 lines
  at 14 px with 3 px to spare, using `px-3` on the bar.
  - *Recommendation:* if a font-loading difference breaks it, drop the name to 13 px (108 px). Do
    not move the language switch into the menu, because that would be a spec change.
- **Pinned bar plus menu panel versus the burst toast and the dragon.** The bar is `z-40` and
  `Burst` already sits above it, so a toast still shows over the bar. Checked on screenshots.
- **The `details` panel stays open after client-side navigation.** This is handled by
  `afterNavigate`. Proven in VERIFY: tap Menu → Journal, and the panel is closed on `/journal`.
- **The opaque chapter banner changes only phones.** Desktop has the same line-through-text flaw,
  but PB-1 freezes desktop.
  - *Recommendation:* leave desktop alone here, and fix it later as a trivial PR if you want it.
- **The 12 px floor moves desktop text by a fraction of a pixel per label.** PB-1 is proven by
  geometry, not pixels: at 1024 and 1280 px, header, nav links, cards and trail have the same
  x and width before and after, and heights within +4 px per card. Side-by-side screenshots show
  the rest.
- **Tables on phones scroll sideways.** That is the approved CB-3, and the page itself still never
  scrolls sideways.
- **Readers' saved progress (PB-5):** localStorage keys and the toggle code are untouched, since
  only markup classes change. VERIFY seeds progress before the deploy-equivalent build and reads it
  after.

## Build notes (deliberate differences, recorded after review 0005 #10)
- **Nav links:** two `{#each links}` loops (desktop pills, menu rows) instead of one `{#snippet}`.
  The markup differs too much for one snippet to stay readable. The list still exists once, as the
  `links` array.
- **Dragon** is kept at 34 px and the **name** set at 13 px, the plan's fallback. That keeps
  "Build Journey" on its second line at 320 px with 4 px to spare. The header measures 57 px
  (CB-1 ≤ 64).
- **Language links** gain `grid place-items-center` at every width. On desktop that is still one
  centred line, and PB-1's geometry probe proves the size is unchanged.
- **Review fixes** (#1, #2, #3, #9, #11): one commit each.
  - #7 adds one line to `docs/testing.md`. That file was outside the plan's list; adding it was
    approved at triage.
