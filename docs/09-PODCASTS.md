## 4. Discovering new podcasts and episodes — tools

| Tool | What it gives you | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **⭐ iTunes Search/Lookup API** — `itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=<id>` | `releaseDate` = **the real latest-episode date**, `trackCount`, `feedUrl` | **Free, no key** — the best tool for this problem. Run it **before** trusting any roundup post |
| **Raw RSS feed** | Full `pubDate` per episode + **sponsor mentions in the show notes are countable** | Free. The sponsor tables above were extracted this way — the best competitor-intel trick here |
| **[Podscan.fm](https://podscan.fm)** | Keyword alerts on **podcast transcripts**. Arvid Kahl's product | **10-day free trial, no card**; then $100/mo (50 alerts) | Set alerts on your app name + category terms + competitor names → every relevant episode arrives within minutes. **The single highest-leverage setup on the page** |
| **[Listen Notes](https://www.listennotes.com/)** | Listen Score (0–100) + percentile, **full-text search of show notes/transcripts** | Web search free; API 300 requests/mo free | Reference: Unofficial Shopify Podcast **LS 50 / top 0.5%**; Liquid Weekly **LS 24 / top 10%** |
| **[Rephonic](https://rephonic.com)** | 4M+ shows, listener estimates, **similar-show graph**, host contact info | $99–299/mo, 7-day trial | Built for **getting booked as a guest**. The similar-show graph is the best way to find rising shows |
| **Apple category charts** | Business → **Entrepreneurship (`?genre=1493`)** and **Marketing (`?genre=1492`)** | Free | ⚠️ **There's no Shopify or e-commerce category** — niche shows don't make the charts |
| [Podnews](https://podnews.net/) | Daily industry newsletter | Free | For spotting new launches early |

⚠️ **Chartable is DEAD** — `chartable.com` → `megaphone.spotify.com`. Spotify shut it down in December 2024.
Every guide that recommends it is stale.
⚠️ **Spotify category charts are unusable** — `/us/business` returns 200 but it's an **empty shell**.
Use Apple for categories.

## 5. Two cross-cutting findings — worth more than any single show

### ① Merchants are replacing SaaS with AI-built internal tools — and saying so on air

It surfaced **independently in five separate feeds** in 2026. Four operator communities that don't
share audiences converging on the same behavior is not noise:

- **Andrew Faris, Apr 2, 2026** — "My COO replaced 7 software tools with one vibe-coded app"
- **eComFuel, Aug 21, 2026** — "Claude Code First Company"; and **Mar 14, 2025** — "How a non-programmer
  cut $150K in SaaS fees with AI"
- **My Wife Quit Her Job, Jul 10, 2026** — "How we got 30 hours a week back with Claude Code"
- **Limited Supply, Jul 29, 2026** — "Stop building landing pages by hand"

> **Every Shopify app whose value amounts to a form and a database is being commoditized right now.**
> This should shape **what you build**, not just how you market it.
> If you listen to nothing else from this page, listen to these five.

## 6. If you only subscribe to five

1. **The Shopify App Show** — exactly your business, and only 26 episodes to catch up on
2. **Shopify1Percent** — an app founder explaining what convinces a merchant to pay
3. **Honest Ecommerce** — your real buyer (6–8 figure merchants), *and* a realistic distribution target
4. **Startups For the Rest of Us** — the craft of the solo software business
5. **Liquid Weekly** — the only place where Shopify platform engineers get on the mic

Then **Operators** one episode a month (for the buy-vs-build signal) and
**Jason & Scot** once a month (purely as platform-risk radar).

1. **Talk Commerce** — very high publishing volume, easiest way in
2. **Ecommerce Coffee Break** — explicitly hosts Shopify app founders and sells to SaaS
3. **Honest Ecommerce** — the midweek vendor-showcase bonus episodes; **the Aug 13, 2026 TestParty
   episode is the template to study**
