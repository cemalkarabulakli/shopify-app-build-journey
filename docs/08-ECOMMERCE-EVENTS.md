## 🚨 The one finding that frames everything: the two-tier price wall

Almost every major e-commerce conference charges **retailers/brands nothing or next to nothing,
and software vendors 3–9× more**.

| Event | Retailer pays | **You (app vendor) pay** | Multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Whalies | $360 | **$3,995** | **11×** |
| Commerce Roundtable | $450 | **$4,000** | 8.9× |
| Prosper Show | $499 | **$4,399–5,000** | 8.8× |
| eTail Palm Springs | $599.50 | **$4,499** | 7.5× |
| CommerceNext | Free (if eligible) | **$4,495** | ∞ |
| NRF Big Show | Free expo pass | **$3,200** | ∞ |
| Shoptalk Spring | Free + $750 travel stipend | $3,245 (**startup $1,795**) | ∞ |

eTail goes even further: non-retailers *"cannot access the event app until the Friday before
the event"* — you pay $4,499 and are **last in line** for messaging.

> **These are sponsorship fees dressed up as tickets.** The model: retailers attend for free,
> **vendors subsidise it.** You are the product.

### Tier 3 — search platforms (how to use them properly)

- **Eventbrite:** `eventbrite.com/d/<location>/<keyword>/` — location is `online` or `<region>--<city>`.
  Working ones: [/d/online/ecommerce/](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/online/ecommerce/) ·
  [/d/online/dtc-brands/](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/online/dtc-brands/) ·
  [/d/united-kingdom--london/ecommerce/](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/united-kingdom--london/ecommerce/).
  Very noisy (dropshipping courses) but worth a saved search per city.
- **Luma:** `lu.ma` → `luma.com`. ⚠️ **There is NO e-commerce/DTC/retail category** — the 12 categories
  only cover Tech, AI, Crypto etc. **Discovery is city-based only.** `luma.com/ecommerce` and
  `luma.com/shopify` return 200 but are **individual event pages, not hubs** — a trap.
  Global search requires a login; use **`site:lu.ma <keyword>` on Google** instead.
  **Best use:** check the city pages during known conference weeks — the good side dinners are there.
- **Meetup:** ⚠️ `meetup.com/topics/e-commerce/` reports **0 groups, 0 members**. Only the
  [Shopify topic page](https://www.meetup.com/topics/shopify/) works.

## 7. Honest summary

The four in-person options flagged as "worth it" add up to **$10–15K + weeks of travel**.
Before spending: **none of these events is where Shopify apps actually get distributed.**
Distribution happens in the App Store listing, in agency/partner relationships, and in Shopify's
own partner ecosystem. **Conferences are a relationship channel with a 12–24 month lag, not a customer acquisition channel.**

**The cheapest proof costs zero:** the Berlin CFP application (free, 18 Sep), the free Shopify
Community AMAs, and talking to 50 Shopify merchants this month. If you can't turn a warm intro
into a paying merchant, a $4,500 badge won't fix that.
