## 🚨 Know this first: there is NO Shopify Payments in Turkey

Verified from Shopify's own help center — **Turkey is not** among the 40 supported countries.
Bulgaria, Romania, Greece, Croatia, Cyprus, Germany **are**.

Turkish merchants can use Shopify (TRY checkout works, the admin is in Turkish) but they have to plug in **iyzico**,
**PayTR** or **Craftgate**; on top of that come ETBİS (Turkey's e-commerce registry), KVKK (Turkey's
data-protection law), İYS (Turkey's commercial-messaging consent system) and e-invoice obligations.

**Commercial consequence:** There are ~15,000 active Shopify stores in Turkey *(StoreLeads, secondary source —
should be treated as an upper bound since it includes dormant stores)*. The mass merchant base is on
Trendyol/Hepsiburada/ikas/Ticimax, not Shopify.

> **Turkish Shopify merchants are the wrong audience to be the first customers of a Shopify app.**
> There are few of them, they're price-sensitive in a depreciating currency, and they're
> structurally underserved by the platform. **Use Turkey as a cheap base and for travel proximity,
> not for customers.** The money is in rooms in Bucharest, Berlin, London and Sofia.

## 4b. What you can attend without flying out of Turkey

> **Hard truth: there is no online Shopify event circuit.** Editions is a web page, DotDev
> is Toronto-only and sells out, and there is no active Shopify meetup group in Istanbul/Sofia/Berlin/Amsterdam.
> Reaching merchants remotely means: **forums, partner webinars and newsletters** — not conferences.

| Channel | URL | Why |
|---|---|---|

   is scoped. Zero cost, highest ceiling.

   Istanbul Partners Meetup #3. **In your own city and not listed anywhere.**
3. **Subscribe today:** community.shopify.dev Announcements · the dotdev.shopify.com signup form ·
   the two Meetup groups (CEE Bucharest, London).
4. **Watch the DotDev 2026 recordings** — free, 2 days of platform/API content.
5. **Decide on London 23–24 Sep** — eCommerce Expo + Shopify Partner Meetup in a single trip.
6. **Wait for the May 2027 Bucharest announcement** — €50, sells out months ahead.

> **Honest warning:** The presence of Shopify's **App Ecosystem** team is not confirmed at any event
> in this file — **except Shopify Connect and the two community meetups (Bucharest, Istanbul).**
> If your goal is **Shopify staff** rather than merchants, Partner Slack/Discord, the Partner Dashboard and
> community.shopify.dev are cheaper and higher-bandwidth than any flight.
>
> And the bigger point: **if you don't have paying users yet, conferences are not your constraint.**
> Everything except the two free items (the Berlin CFP, the Connect email) should wait until
> merchants are already paying you for something.
